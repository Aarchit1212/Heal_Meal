Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What HealMeal stores

HealMeal stores chat history and a randomly generated local identifier in your browser’s local storage so that your conversation remains available on that device. This information is not sent to a HealMeal database.

How your requests are handled

When you send a chat question or generate a meal plan, the request is sent to the HealMeal server and then to its AI provider to generate a response. Do not enter sensitive personal information, such as medical records, account numbers, or government identification numbers.

Microphone access

Voice transcription uses your browser’s speech-recognition feature. HealMeal asks for microphone permission only when you choose to use the microphone. You can revoke that permission in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can clear saved chat history at any time by clearing this site’s browser data. You may also use the app without voice input.

Health information notice

HealMeal is a general health and nutrition information tool, not a medical provider. Please avoid sharing information you would not want processed by an AI service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The updated date above will show when it was last revised.
